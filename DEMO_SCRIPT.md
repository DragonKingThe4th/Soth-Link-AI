# Soth Link AI — 3-Minute Demo Script

## 0:00–0:25 — The problem
“Every family has stories that exist only because someone remembers them. Soth Link AI is designed to keep those stories from becoming contextless fragments. It is a digital heirloom for memories, values, traditions, and historical experience.”

## 0:25–0:50 — Create a capsule
Open Soth Link AI and click **Create a Time Capsule**. Create a capsule for a person or historical subject. Enter the title, subject name, recipient or research purpose, and a short description.

Say: “The product starts with a simple question: who are we preserving? We do not ask users to fill out a complicated archive schema.”

## 0:50–1:15 — Record a memory
Open the capsule and click **Add Memory**. Choose a category such as Childhood or Wisdom. Enter a prompt and a written response, then optionally add an era and tags.

Say: “The memory itself stays in the user’s words. Metadata is optional so the act of preserving feels natural.”

## 1:15–1:40 — Guided AI interview
Open **Guided interview**. Select a category and generate a question. Show the one-question-at-a-time experience and the fallback behavior if AI is unavailable.

Say: “Gemini is used as a gentle interviewer. The server prompt tells it to stay grounded in the saved archive rather than inventing biography.”

## 1:40–2:05 — Legacy chat
Open **Legacy chat** and ask: “What values mattered most?” Show the answer and cited memory entries.

Say: “The chat is not a generic chatbot. It is a memory-grounded reader. If the archive does not contain an answer, the system is designed to say so.”

## 2:05–2:30 — AI insights
Open **AI insights** and generate the reflection cards. Show wisdom themes, turning points, relationship or cultural values, and timeline/topic summary.

Say: “The insights layer helps a future reader see patterns without replacing the original memories.”

## 2:30–2:50 — Legacy letter
Open **Legacy letter**, choose a tone such as Heartfelt, and generate the letter. Show Copy, Print, and Export controls.

Say: “Finally, Soth Link AI can turn the recorded archive into a keepsake letter. It is clearly labeled as AI-generated from the saved record.”

## 2:50–3:00 — Security and closing
Say: “This version is local-first: capsules are persisted in browser storage. Gemini calls happen only through the Express backend, and the API key is kept server-side. The app is Vercel-ready.”

Close with: “Because preserving a life is not about saving everything. It is about keeping the things that help someone understand what mattered.”
